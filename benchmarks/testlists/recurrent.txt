# The two fused-step lanes against the tensor-core GEMM they replace.  Each arm
# is a 64-step dependent chain in one CUDA graph, which is the only protocol
# that resolves a 2-12 us kernel -- a single call costs more than the step.  No
# --refcheck: the cuBLAS arm is a lower bound, not an equivalent, and the
# numerics are checked against FP32 equations in tests/kernels/.
--family recurrent --subroutine lstm_step_cute --backends cute cxx cublas --dtype float16 --iters 20 --warmup-iters 5
--family recurrent --subroutine lstm_step_cute --backends cute cxx cublas --dtype bfloat16 --iters 20 --warmup-iters 5
